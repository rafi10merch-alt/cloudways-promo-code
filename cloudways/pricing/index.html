What actually moves your bill

01

Server size

RAM and vCPU tier is the biggest lever — traffic spikes usually mean scaling this up.

02

Bandwidth overage

Each plan includes a bandwidth allowance; sustained overage is billed per GB.

03

Add-ons

Dedicated backups beyond the free retention window, CloudwaysCDN, and extra staging apps add to the base rate.

Premium vs Standard tiers

Cloudways' newer "Premium" tier (built on its own infrastructure) sits alongside the original "Standard" DigitalOcean/Vultr/Linode/AWS/GCP tiers. Premium targets higher baseline performance and simplified scaling; Standard gives more provider choice at typically lower entry cost. Which is cheaper depends on your traffic profile — compare both against your expected monthly visits before deciding.
