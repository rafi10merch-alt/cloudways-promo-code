Cloudways pricing: how the plans actually work

Cloudways doesn't sell one fixed "hosting plan" — you pick an underlying cloud provider (DigitalOcean, Vultr, Linode/Akamai, AWS, or Google Cloud), a server size on that provider, and pay hourly or monthly for what you use, on top of Cloudways' management layer.
