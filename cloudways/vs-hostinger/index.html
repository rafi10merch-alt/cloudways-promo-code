Cloudways vs Hostinger: Which Fits Your Site?

Short version: Hostinger is cheaper shared/cloud hosting with a simpler dashboard. Cloudways is managed cloud infrastructure with more headroom, more control, and a higher entry price. Which wins depends on traffic and how hands-on you want to be.

Pricing at different traffic levels

At low traffic (a few thousand visits/month), Hostinger's flat-rate plans are usually cheaper outright. As traffic and resource needs grow — particularly for WooCommerce stores — Cloudways' scalable server sizing tends to close or reverse that gap, because you're paying for actual compute rather than jumping shared-hosting tiers.

Choose Cloudways if…

  • You run WooCommerce or a high-traffic WordPress site
  • You want a choice of underlying cloud provider
  • You need staging, team access, and granular server control

Choose Hostinger if…

  • You're hosting a small blog or first website
  • Budget is the primary constraint
  • You want an all-in-one beginner dashboard, no server concepts

FAQ

Is Cloudways more expensive than Hostinger?

At entry level, yes — Cloudways starts around $14/mo usage-based versus Hostinger's sub-$5 intro shared plans. The gap narrows as traffic and resource needs grow.

Can I migrate from Hostinger to Cloudways?

Yes, Cloudways supports migration via its free migrator plugin or a paid white-glove migration for complex sites. See our WordPress setup guide for the workflow.
