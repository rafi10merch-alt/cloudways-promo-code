Cloudways Free Trial: What To Expect

Cloudways offers a no-credit-card trial period on select plans so you can test the platform before paying. Exact length and eligibility are set by Cloudways and can change — confirm current terms on their sign-up page.

What's typically included

  • Full access to the Cloudways console and app management tools
  • A live server on your chosen cloud provider at trial-tier specs
  • Access to staging, backups, and the Breeze caching plugin

What happens when the trial ends

You'll be prompted to add billing details to keep the server running. If you don't, the server and its data are typically suspended and later removed after a grace period — export or back up anything you built before the trial ends.

Trial vs. referral credit

These are two different things. The free trial is a time-boxed evaluation period with no payment required upfront. Referral credit (see our promo code page) is account credit applied after you sign up through a tracked link and add billing — the two aren't mutually exclusive, but they solve different problems.
