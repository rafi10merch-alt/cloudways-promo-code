Why you won't find a real "50% off" code

Cloudways sells compute by the hour on a pay-as-you-go model, sourced from DigitalOcean, Vultr, Linode/Akamai, AWS, and Google Cloud. Because the underlying cost is metered infrastructure, not a fixed retail SKU, there's little for a blanket percentage-off coupon to discount — which is part of why you mostly see referral links and time-boxed credit promotions instead of evergreen codes in this space.

If a listing elsewhere claims a specific static code, the way to verify it is simple: go to Cloudways' sign-up page and see whether that code field actually changes your invoiced total. Screenshots and third-party claims aren't proof; the checkout page is.

What's realistic to expect

  • Referral credit — Cloudways periodically offers new users an account credit (amount and duration change) when they sign up through a partner link.
  • Free trial — a no-card trial period to test the platform before committing. See our free trial breakdown.
  • Seasonal promotions — occasionally run around major sales periods, announced on Cloudways' own site first.

FAQ

Common questions

Does Cloudways have an official discount code?

Cloudways does not run a permanent public discount-code program. What's consistently available is a tracked referral link (like the one on this page) and occasional time-limited sign-up promotions that Cloudways runs directly on its own pricing page. If you see a site claiming a permanent "50% off forever" code, treat it as unverified until you can confirm it on cloudways.com at checkout.

Is a referral link the same as a coupon?

No, and we don't want to blur that line. A coupon is issued by the merchant and applies a discount automatically. A referral link is a tracked URL that credits the referring site with a commission if you sign up — it doesn't change your price unless Cloudways is separately running a sign-up credit promotion at that time, which it sometimes does.

Will using this link cost me more?

No. Referral tracking doesn't add anything to your bill. You pay Cloudways' standard published rate for the plan and usage you choose, whether you arrive via a referral link or by typing the URL directly.

How do I know the current sign-up offer?

Cloudways' own pricing page is the source of truth, since promotions change without notice. We link to it from this page and update our notes when we notice a change, but always confirm the live number before you enter payment details.
