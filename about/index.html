About CloudSavings

CloudSavings is an independent site focused specifically on Cloudways hosting — pricing, setup, and honest comparisons against alternatives. We are not Cloudways, and we're not neutral about disclosing that we earn a commission when you sign up through our links.

Who writes this

Content is produced and fact-checked by the CloudSavings editorial team. Where we describe hands-on testing (like our full review), that reflects an actual provisioned server run over a real testing period, not a summary of someone else's marketing material.

How we make money

We earn a commission if you sign up for Cloudways through a referral link on this site. This does not change your price. Full detail is in our affiliate disclosure. It does not affect what we report about performance, support, or pricing — see our editorial policy for how we keep those separate.

What we don't do

  • We don't publish fabricated reviews, ratings, or testimonials.
  • We don't claim a discount code exists when it doesn't — see our promo code page for how we handle that specifically.
  • We don't hide the affiliate relationship in fine print; it's disclosed on every page closest to a referral CTA.
