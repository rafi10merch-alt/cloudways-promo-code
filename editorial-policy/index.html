Editorial Policy

Fact-checking

Pricing, specs, and feature claims are checked against Cloudways' own current documentation and pricing pages at the time of writing. Articles show a "last updated" date so you can judge freshness.

Hands-on testing

Content labeled "review" or "tested" reflects direct use of the platform for a real workload, not repackaged marketing copy.

Corrections

If something is inaccurate or out of date, contact us and we'll review and correct it, noting the update date on the page.

No fake reviews

We do not publish fabricated testimonials, star ratings, or user reviews. Any opinions expressed are the author's own assessment, clearly attributed.

Commercial relationships don't set conclusions

Our Cloudways referral relationship (see affiliate disclosure) doesn't determine what we recommend. Comparison pages present competitors' genuine strengths, including where they beat Cloudways.
