Cloudways Review 2026: Three Months in Production

By Amara Chen, Hosting & Infrastructure Editor · Published Feb 1, 2026 · Updated Aug 1, 2026

This review covers a WooCommerce store moved to Cloudways on a DigitalOcean-backed plan, tracked over three months of normal trading traffic. It's not a lab benchmark — it's what showed up in real uptime, support tickets, and the monthly invoice.

Setup experience

Launching the app took under ten minutes from account creation to a live server with WordPress and WooCommerce installed. DNS propagation and the SSL certificate issuance were the slowest steps, which is normal for any host.

Performance

With Breeze's full-page cache and Redis object caching enabled, product pages loaded consistently under two seconds on a mid-tier plan. The exceptions were pages hitting live cart/session data, which bypass cache by design — expected behavior, not a flaw.

Support

Live chat responses during the trial period averaged a few minutes for general questions; a server-level issue escalated to a ticket took closer to half a day to fully resolve. That's in line with what's typical for managed cloud hosts at this price point, not exceptional in either direction.

Pricing in practice

The invoice tracked closely with the estimate on the pricing page, with the main variable being bandwidth overage during a promotional traffic spike. Budgeting a buffer above the advertised base rate is worth doing for any store running sales campaigns.

What worked

  • Fast, low-friction app launch
  • Caching stack handled real WooCommerce traffic well
  • Staging made plugin updates low-risk

What didn't

  • Entry pricing is higher than budget shared hosts
  • CDN is a separate add-on, not bundled by default
  • Deep server-level tickets took longer than chat questions

Verdict

For a WooCommerce store outgrowing shared hosting, Cloudways held up well on both performance and support during this test window. If you're comparing it against a budget host, see our Cloudways vs Hostinger breakdown for the pricing trade-off in more detail.
